# GCD of Two Numbers

**Difficulty**: Easy

## Problem Statement

Given two positive integers `n1` and `n2`, find their Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) / Highest Common Factor (HCF).

The GCD of two integers is the largest positive integer that divides both numbers without leaving a remainder.

## Examples

### Example 1:

```
Input: n1 = 9, n2 = 12
Output: 3
Explanation: Factors of 9 are 1, 3, 9. Factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. The greatest common factor is 3.
```

### Example 2:

```
Input: n1 = 20, n2 = 15
Output: 5
```

## Constraints

- `1 <= n1, n2 <= 10^9`
