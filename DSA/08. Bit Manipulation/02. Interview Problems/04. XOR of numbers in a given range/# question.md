# Find XOR of Numbers in Range [L, R]

**Difficulty**: Medium

## Problem Statement

Given two integers `L` and `R`, calculate the bitwise XOR of all integers in the range `[L, R]` inclusive.

## Examples

### Example 1:
```
Input: L = 4, R = 8
Output: 8
Explanation: 4 ^ 5 ^ 6 ^ 7 ^ 8 = 8.
```

## Constraints

- `1 <= L <= R <= 10^9`
